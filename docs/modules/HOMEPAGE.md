# Homepage Structure & Section Specifications

## HopeHorizon / Vikas NGO Ecosystem — Phase 5

The HopeHorizon Homepage (`/`) consists of 20 distinct, content-driven sections structured to maximize trust, engagement, and conversion:

---

### Section Inventory

1. **Announcement Bar (`AnnouncementBar.tsx`)**:
   - Urgent field appeals, emergency alerts, dismissible banner with direct donation trigger.
2. **Sticky Header & Mega-Navigation (`Navbar.tsx`)**:
   - Adaptive background transition on scroll (glassmorphic dark slate), quick Donate CTA, search modal shortcut (`Cmd+K`), and accessible mobile drawer.
3. **Hero Section (`HeroSection.tsx`)**:
   - Emotion-stirring humanitarian headline: *"Empowering Marginalized Communities Through Dignity, Clean Water & Equal Education"*.
   - Dual CTAs: *Donate to Active Appeals* and *Explore Our Work*.
4. **Trust & Proof Strip (`TrustStatsBar.tsx`)**:
   - Section 80G tax certificate badge, FCRA clearance badge, and NITI Aayog Darpan accreditation ID.
5. **Who We Are (`AboutPreviewSection.tsx`)**:
   - Concise introduction to the foundation's origins, grassroots philosophy, and 100% secular community mandate.
6. **Mission & Vision (`MissionVisionSection.tsx`)**:
   - Clear twin vision statements targeting 2030 sustainable development goals.
7. **Core Values (`ValuesSection.tsx`)**:
   - Integrity, Radical Transparency, Community Ownership, Gender Equity, Scientific Rigor, and Dignity.
8. **Action Areas (`ActionAreasSection.tsx`)**:
   - Visual breakdown of our 4 thematic intervention sectors.
9. **Featured Programs (`FeaturedProgramsSection.tsx`)**:
   - Dynamic cards highlighting Clean Water, STEM Education, Women's Livelihoods, and Rapid Disaster Response.
10. **Featured Grassroots Projects (`FeaturedProjectsSection.tsx`)**:
    - Ground installations with live progress thermometers, milestone tracking, and geotagged village locations.
11. **Urgent Priority Appeal (`UrgentCampaignSection.tsx`)**:
    - Time-sensitive crowdfunding campaign with target amount, verified raised total, and countdown meter.
12. **Why Trust HopeHorizon (`WhySupportUsSection.tsx`)**:
    - Direct-to-beneficiary metrics (88.4% field utilization), Deloitte independent audit, and open-source Google Sheets ledger integration.
13. **Measurable Ground Impact (`ImpactMetricsSection.tsx`)**:
    - Ground outcomes: 115,000+ people reached, 96 solar water plants, 142 schools equipped, 12,400 women trained.
14. **Founder & Leadership Message (`FounderSection.tsx`)**:
    - Message from Dr. Evelyn Vance regarding long-term community stewardship.
15. **Verified Success Stories (`SuccessStoriesSection.tsx`)**:
    - Human-centered before/after transformations with safeguarding consent.
16. **Get Involved Call to Action (`VolunteerCtaSection.tsx`)**:
    - Action pathways for student volunteers, medical camp leads, and corporate CSR sponsors.
17. **Upcoming Community Events (`UpcomingEventsSection.tsx`)**:
    - Field distribution drives, volunteer inductions, and public annual townhall dates.
18. **Latest News & Research (`NewsBlogSection.tsx`)**:
    - Verified field dispatches, policy briefs, and photojournalism essays.
19. **Field Moments Gallery (`FieldGallerySection.tsx`)**:
    - Responsive photo grid with lightbox inspection.
20. **Partners, CSR & Institutional Supporters (`PartnersSection.tsx` & `CsrSection.tsx`)**:
    - Partner logos and Section 135 CSR compliance disclosures.
21. **Radical Transparency & Reports (`TransparencySection.tsx`)**:
    - Quick download links to audited balance sheets and Form 10BD statements.
22. **Community Voices & Testimonials (`TestimonialsSection.tsx`)**:
    - Testimonials from village panchayat leads, teacher coordinators, and long-term patrons.
23. **Frequently Asked Questions (`FaqSection.tsx`)**:
    - Accordion addressing 80G tax rebate claims, foreign donation rules, and volunteer onboarding.
24. **Newsletter Subscription (`NewsletterSection.tsx`)**:
    - Validated email capture with instant confirmation toast and duplicate protection.
25. **Final Emotional CTA (`FinalCtaSection.tsx`)**:
    - High-impact closing statement driving recurring patron and donor conversions.
26. **Comprehensive Footer (`Footer.tsx`)**:
    - Four-column navigational matrix, legal links, statutory badges, and copyright.
